I recently came across this post by [James Mahy](https://www.linkedin.com/in/james-mahy/) and it got me thinking: is everything AI slop now?

<iframe src="https://www.linkedin.com/embed/feed/update/urn:li:share:7516852924606681088?collapsed=1" width="504" height="264" loading="lazy" title="Bearly Fit LinkedIn post" class="embed"></iframe>

We've gotten to a point where the benchmark is always "make it human, it shouldn't look AI-generated". But what exactly is the definition of AI-generated?

In the early days of these models, it was purple gradients, those glowing buttons, big-ass shadows, rounded borders, and em dashes. Then came the [frontend design skill](https://github.com/anthropics/skills/tree/main/skills/frontend-design) from Claude, followed by a bunch of other design and taste skills, and we slowly started to identify these "AI tells" and cut them out to make the output "feel" more human.

The days of purple gradients are over. But AI slop keeps changing. Six months ago it was em dashes. Now it's those eyebrows Claude likes to add in all caps above every heading, that grotesque font, and the warm editorial palette. We trained the models to stop doing the obvious sloppy stuff, and they just converged on a new common output. Someone could have unknowingly added those eyebrows on their website, but our first instinct would be: "Yeah, this is probably one-shotted by Claude".

If the goal is to make AI output look human, what happens when it starts doing that?

It BECOMES the new slop.

A design that was fine six months ago is slop now because every agent is producing it. And this cycle keeps on repeating.

And it'll only get worse as agents get better at replicating UIs. Any UI a human designed can now be copied with just a screenshot. As more and more people replicate these designs, it'll be even harder to tell or define what slop looks like. Real human work will trigger the same alarms as AI-generated content.

I'm not sure how this cycle ends, or how we actually identify real "craft" moving forward. It's an interesting time to be in. Depressing and fascinating at the same time.
