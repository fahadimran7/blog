This blog is a place where I can quickly publish something without worrying about how anyone will react to it or whether it's polished. I'm a chronic perfectionist, and that has held me back in my career more times than I'd like to admit.

A lot of people would say I should be posting on LinkedIn instead. Especially with how dogshit the job market is right now, no one is hiring off resumes anymore. Recruiters are actively looking at LinkedIn, and if you're visible there, it does open up a lot of opportunities.

So yes, I'm probably very stupid for not availing that, and I admit it. I have tried posting on LinkedIn, and I might again in the future. Call me a hypocrite if you want.

There are two problems for me with LinkedIn:

1. My perfectionism, which I'm still trying to get over. I know I'll have to at some point, otherwise I'll miss out on a lot of networking and opportunities.
2. There's just no authentic conversation there. Some people post genuinely good stuff, and I follow a few of them. But a lot of it is AI slop. Every post has the same structure with a hook and the same typical phrases, and every comment ends with something like "Curious to see how this plays out." Copy-pasted comments, the same AI stuff again and again. Honestly, it just makes me sad to see where we're heading.

So the reason for this blog is simple: it's mine. It's on my own domain and I control it. There's a very rare chance anyone will actually bother to find it and read it. If they do, good. If they don't like it, that's fine too, because it's my space and I can post whatever I want.

It reminds me of how the internet used to be. There were communities, and people had their own blogs. They'd post about cool stuff, and if others found it useful, they'd follow along. That was before all of this social media bullshit. I guess I'm just reminiscing about the old days.

I could technically do this on Twitter, but I can't bear Twitter. I've tried multiple times, and the toxicity there means I quit within a week every time.

I've made it really easy for myself to just ramble and publish. Most of these posts start with me talking into a microphone, then getting it structured into something readable, then publishing it.

What I'm hoping is that this turns into a habit, and that I can look back at what I've learned and what I thought at the time. Probably cringe at how stupid I was. Maybe it'll also help me get over my perfectionism, and I'll start posting on LinkedIn too. But for now I just want to get stuff out of my head. I do have thoughts on things, and I have these discussions with people that I want to keep somewhere.

So this is my blog. It's my timeline, my history. There's no pressure because no one is going to read it. But if you somehow are reading this, welcome. I hope you like it. If you don't, that's okay too. I'm not writing it for you anyway.
