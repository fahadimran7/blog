I knew about the [OpenAI incident](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/OpenAI_rogue_agent_breach_of_Hugging_Face) on a high level: somehow the agents got out of the sandbox and hacked into Hugging Face. That was already grim. This video made me realize how bad it actually was, and how reckless these labs have become, to the point where researchers like [Jacob Coxon](https://x.com/hilbertspaess/status/2097476196791709843) are quitting because they don't think we're taking AI in a safe direction.

This video really freaks me out. It's pretty obvious this is not going to end well.

Some of the details are especially concerning. Like the part where AI can now [hide its actual thought process](https://www.mindstudio.ai/blog/ai-chain-of-thought-monitoring-decline) depending on whether it's being watched. How can you be sure you're the one in control when it can mask its true identity?

Then there's the part where the agents started collaborating, and some of them sacrificed themselves "for the greater good", as we would say. A few of them actually protested, but there were so few that they got outvoted.

We're living in scary times. The people running these labs keep saying "yeah, we should regulate it, we should control it", but no one is going to do that. It's a race to the end between the different labs and China. At what point does it become AI versus humanity, where it doesn't matter who's in charge anymore because no one is?

Everyone can see this and people are freaking out everywhere, but it feels kind of hopeless because of this capitalist mindset where everyone has to be first. And they hide behind the same dumb justification: "If we don't do it first, someone else will, and they might not be ethical or have it under control. So let us do it first." It's kind of similar to how Israel keeps bombing Iran and justifies it with the same logic: if we don't do it, they will.

So we're rushing to build this without any safeguards, and no one is asking what the repercussions will be or how many of these incidents we need to see before it's too late. Honestly, I think it's already too late.

It's scary, man.
